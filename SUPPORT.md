# Hong Kong Mahjong Support

Hong Kong Mahjong is a single-player game of Hong Kong–style mahjong: play against three
computer opponents, claim discards, declare kongs, and win hands scored with the traditional
faan system.

## Contact

For questions, bug reports, or feedback: **oppositecoastllc@gmail.com**

We aim to respond within a few days.

## Common questions

**How is a hand scored?**
A win needs at least 3 faan. A hand is worth 2^faan points (3 faan → 8, 4 → 16, 5 → 32,
6 → 64, 7 or more → 128). Tap the rules button during a game to see the full faan table, and
after each win you'll see a breakdown of the faan earned and the points paid.

**Why can't I win with my hand?**
Your hand may be complete but worth fewer than 3 faan, the minimum needed to win. Check the faan
table in the rules screen for ways to add faan (for example, a concealed hand, all pungs, one
suit, or dragon pungs).

**How do I claim a discard (chow, pung, kong, or win)?**
When another player discards a tile you can use, claim buttons appear briefly. Tap one to claim
the tile, or let the timer run out / tap pass to skip. If several chow combinations are
possible, you'll be asked to choose one.

**How do I turn the sound off?**
Tap the speaker icon at the top of the game screen. Your choice is remembered.

**How do I start over?**
Choose "New Session" in the game. This resets the scores and history for the current session.

**Why do I see ads?**
Ads keep the game free. They appear between hands, never in the middle of play. Whether ads are
personalized depends on the tracking choice you made, which you can change in iOS Settings →
Privacy & Security → Tracking.

**Is this gambling?**
No. Points in the game have no monetary value and can't be bought, sold, or redeemed.

**How do I remove my data?**
All game data and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.
