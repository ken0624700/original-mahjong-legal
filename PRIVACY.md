# Hong Kong Mahjong Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 7, 2026**

Hong Kong Mahjong ("the app") is developed by Opposite Coast. This policy explains what data the
app handles and why. Hong Kong Mahjong is a single-player game: you play Hong Kong–style mahjong
against three computer-controlled opponents on your device.

## Data stored only on your device

- **Game state.** Your current session, scores, and hand history are kept on your device while you
  play and are never sent to our servers.
- **Your settings.** Preferences such as whether sound is muted.

Hong Kong Mahjong does not require an account, does not use your location, and does not access
your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

## Analytics

Hong Kong Mahjong uses TelemetryDeck to understand basic, anonymous usage of the app (for example,
that the app was opened, or that a hand was finished and whether it ended in a win or a draw) so we
can improve it. This is tied to a randomly generated identifier stored on your device, not to your
name, email, or any other personal information, and cannot be used to identify you.

## Advertising

Hong Kong Mahjong shows ads via Google AdMob to support free use of the app. When you first open
the app, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether Hong Kong Mahjong and its ad
partners may track you across other companies' apps and websites for advertising purposes.

You can decline this prompt; the app will still show ads, just less personalized ones. Google
AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers as part of serving ads — see
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for how Google handles this data.

## What we don't do

- We don't require you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, email, or password.
- We don't sell your personal data.
- Hong Kong Mahjong is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from
  children.
- The app does not involve real-money gambling. Points shown in the game have no monetary value
  and cannot be purchased, exchanged, or redeemed.

## Your choices

- Decline tracking/personalized ads via the prompt described above. You can also change your
  tracking choice later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game data and settings from your device.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we
do.
